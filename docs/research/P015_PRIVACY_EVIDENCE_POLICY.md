# P015 privacy and evidence policy

Keep raw scan output local and access-controlled. Never publish MAC addresses, device paths, serial numbers, names, private IPs, raw advertisements, keys, pairing secrets, usernames, or transcripts. Review even aggregate logs for identifying combinations. Label software fixtures separately from physical measurements. Record unknown or missing evidence as blocked or not_run; never infer RF, identity, cleanup or Watch qualification from a synthetic test. Authorization remains `never_evaluated`.
