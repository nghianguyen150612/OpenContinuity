## Licensing policy for OpenContinuity

The repository's original MIT license remains in force. Keep the portable core and default binaries free of GPLv3 code and dependencies unless maintainers explicitly choose and document a compatible distribution strategy after legal review. GPLv3 source copied into or linked with a combined work can require distribution of corresponding source under GPL terms; a subprocess boundary is not by itself a guaranteed legal safe harbor. GPL-2.0 is a separate license and is not automatically interchangeable with GPLv3. LGPL-2.1 permits some forms of library use subject to its conditions, notices and user relinking/replacement rights. MIT sources still require their notices and attribution. Unknown licenses are not reusable.

P001 includes no upstream source, binaries, assets, packet captures or secrets. Before adding any dependency, recheck its exact pinned revision, source and bundled asset licenses, SPDX expression, transitive dependencies, notices, linking model, packaging and update policy. Preserve attribution and license texts where required.
